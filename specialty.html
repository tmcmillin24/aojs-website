Specialty Services

Specialty Cleaning Services

Professional carpet cleaning, floor stripping and waxing, and pressure washing services for businesses and facilities throughout our service area.

Deep Carpet Care

Carpet Cleaning

Our carpet cleaning services are designed to remove embedded dirt and grime and help keep high-traffic areas looking clean and professional. We use professional carpet-cleaning equipment to provide a deeper clean for carpets and area rugs throughout your facility.

Scheduled carpet cleaning can also be incorporated into an ongoing facility cleaning program.
Professional Floor Care

Stripping & Waxing

Different flooring materials require different cleaning products, stripping methods, and finishes. Our team provides professional stripping and waxing services designed to restore the appearance of worn, high-traffic floors and help maintain a clean, polished finish.

Floor-care service can be customized around the flooring, traffic level, and maintenance needs of your facility.
Exterior Cleaning

Pressure Washing

Exterior surfaces and exposed areas naturally accumulate dirt and buildup over time. All Over Janitorial Services provides professional pressure washing to clean exterior surfaces and help restore a cleaner, well-maintained appearance to your property.

Recurring pressure-washing service can also be scheduled when ongoing exterior maintenance is needed.
